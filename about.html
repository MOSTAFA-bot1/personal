How I work

Clarity first, then craft.

I like interfaces that make sense before they try to impress. That means clear hierarchy, readable type, responsive behavior, and small interactions that help people move forward.

My work is grounded in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I learn by shipping, reviewing what I built, and making the next version more thoughtful than the last.

Working principles

Make it understandable

Good structure helps users and makes future changes less expensive.

Respect every screen

Responsive design is part of the idea, not a final polish pass.

Keep learning in public

Each project is a chance to practice, document, and improve.
